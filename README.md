## 🚀 Featured Engineering Projects

### 1. Impact of EV Charging on Distribution Grids
**Tools:** MATLAB, Simulink, Power-System Analysis

- Study of EV charging impacts on IEEE 33-bus and 69-bus distribution systems.
- Analysis across seven loading scenarios using backward-forward sweep load flow.
- Investigation of voltage deviations and system losses.
- Grey Wolf Optimizer-based solar distributed-generation sizing.

**Reported study results:** Loss reductions of 89.8% and 97.9% in the
respective studied cases, with bus voltages restored to the
0.95–1.05 p.u. range.

### 2. Smart Load Monitoring and Fault Detection
**Tools:** MATLAB, Simulink

- Distribution-network monitoring and RMS-based fault detection.
- Study of line-to-ground, line-to-line, and three-phase fault conditions.
- Detection logic for voltage sag and swell.
- Exploration of debounce logic, selective isolation, and structured alerts.

### 3. Transistor-Based Temperature-Controlled Fan
**Tools:** Electronics, Transistor Switching

- Designed a temperature-responsive fan-control circuit.
- Explored transistor switching for automatic control based on temperature.
